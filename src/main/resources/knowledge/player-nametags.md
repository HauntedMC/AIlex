Use `/nametag selfview on`, `/nametag selfview off`, `/nametag selfview toggle`, or `/nametag selfview status` to control whether you see your own floating nametag, where available.

The setting normally persists across compatible HauntedMC backends. Your tag can be hidden temporarily when you are too far away, changing world, dead, spectating, hidden, or gliding with Elytra; that does not necessarily change your saved self-view preference.
