## Conduct, language, and advertising
Respect staff and other players. Do not spam, swear at or bully people, discriminate, evade text filters, act toxically, or advertise. Dutch and English are the server languages; German is tolerated. Threatening or carrying out DDoS attacks is prohibited. Names and nicknames must be readable, appropriate, and must not imitate another player to confuse, hinder, or scam.

## Fair play and AFK play
Use an unmodified Minecraft client unless a mod, client, or hardware extension is explicitly permitted. Do not hack, glitch, or exploit server mistakes; report them to staff. Do not bypass idle kicks, use automatic anti-AFK constructions, deliberately reset the idle timer, or keep farms active with alt accounts. Ask staff if you are unsure whether a modification is allowed.

## Builds, farms, claims, and structures
Do not grief, claim another player's build, or make inappropriate builds. You are responsible for who you trust in a claim or plot. Claims and plots whose owner has been absent for more than 100 days may be released. Follow the entity limits. Raid farms, bad-omen farms, 0-tick farms, duplication bugs, and chunk loaders are prohibited. Do not claim whole biomes without an active project or loot structures on a large scale; ask staff about administrator-owned structures.

## PvP and economy
Spawn killing and account boosting through repeated kills are prohibited. Deathtraps—killing players outside PvP areas, including with falls or lava—are prohibited. Scamming with any currency is prohibited. Do not trade in-game items or currencies for external value.

## Reports, staff, and sanctions
Do not make deliberate false accusations in the forum or game chat. Follow staff instructions; objections and unban/unmute requests must be raised afterwards through the official support/forum route. Do not evade a ban or mute with another identity or account. The official rules state that the owner makes the final binding decision in disputes. A violation can result in a warning, kick, mute, or ban; see the published punishment overview for the applicable standard durations.

Use `/rules` or `/regels` for the published in-game rule information; the current official rules page remains authoritative.
