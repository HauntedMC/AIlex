The current Global Rank page lists skins from Elite. Use `/skin <Minecraft name>` to temporarily use that account's official skin, or `/skin remove` to restore your own skin. The donor name must be a normal Minecraft account name.

Skin changes work only while you are online and can have a cooldown. They are not saved across reconnecting or switching backend. A failed account lookup does not change your skin.
