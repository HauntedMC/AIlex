HauntedMC has SimplePets installed. Use `/pet` for the player help and currently unlocked options. Its normal player controls include `/pet list` to see available pet types, `/pet summon <type>` to summon one, `/pet gui` for the pet menu, `/pet rename` to change its name, and `/pet remove` to dismiss it.

The types, number of active pets, cosmetic unlocks, names, movement, and world restrictions are set by HauntedMC. A pet being installed does not mean every type or feature is available to every player, and a cosmetic pet does not give you claim access, storage, combat power, or an exception to entity limits.
