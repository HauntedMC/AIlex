HauntedMC's published privacy policy applies to the website, Minecraft servers, and store. It says visitor data may be retained anonymously, cookies are used for site functionality, and customer data is used to process orders and related communication. The policy directs questions to HauntedMC through its website. Do not ask for or disclose private player data in chat.
