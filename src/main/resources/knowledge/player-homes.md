Homes are available on Survival and Creative. Use `/homes` to view your homes, `/sethome <name>` to create one, and `/delhome <name>` to remove one. Your bed can also act as a spawn point.

The current rank pages list 2 homes for Speler, 10 for Elite, 15 for Legend, and 40 for Supreme and Supreme+. A home can still fail if its destination is unavailable or a current game-mode rule prevents teleporting.
