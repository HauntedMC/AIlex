- HauntedMC is a Dutch Minecraft community. Players can join with `play.hauntedmc.nl`.
- Official public sites are `https://hauntedmc.nl` and `https://store.hauntedmc.nl`.
- The active game-mode categories are Survival, Creative and Minigames. Skyblock is not an active HauntedMC game mode.
- Events and updates are announced through HauntedMC's official channels.
- Dutch and English are supported in player-facing help. Keep answers focused on HauntedMC rather than making claims about other servers.
- For an unknown or time-sensitive server detail, direct a player to `/help`, `/ranks`, the official site, the Store, or staff instead of guessing.
