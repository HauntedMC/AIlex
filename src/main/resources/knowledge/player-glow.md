Use `/glow` to open the glow selection and `/glow remove` to clear it. Available effects can include the sixteen Minecraft team colours plus animated `rainbow` and `hauntedmc` effects; the menu only permits effects you have access to.

Glow applies immediately. Your selected glow can be saved for use on other compatible HauntedMC backends, although an unavailable permission or a temporary database problem can prevent it from restoring later.
