## Game modes over time
HauntedMC has advertised or operated more game modes over its history than are active now. Old public material and third-party directories contain names such as Skyblock, Factions, Prison and KitPvP. Current verified categories are only Survival, Creative and Minigames; historical mode names should be used only when answering a question about the past.

## Old ranks and products
Historical store/order traces can contain rank/product names that are absent from the current canonical rank list, including `God`. Current public player ranks are Speler, Elite, Legend, Supreme and Supreme+. A historical purchase name is not enough to reintroduce a retired rank.

## Old rule specifics
Older HauntedMC rules contained details that the 23 June 2026 rules no longer state in the same way, including automatic tolerance of German, a blanket six-month maximum for temporary punishment, exact portal/farm counts, specific old raid/bad-omen/flying-machine restrictions and older forum-only appeal language. Do not quote those specifics as current rules unless a newer game-mode-specific rule or `/limits` independently confirms them.

## Old version/world information
Historical news mentions Minecraft 1.20.4, older world-border expansions and version-specific vanilla changes. These are useful historical facts but must never answer “what version/world border does HauntedMC have now?” Current version and border are live/configuration-sensitive.
