Everyone can sit on blocks by right-clicking them and use `/sit` or `/sit toggle`. Elite and higher can sit on other players and use `/sit playertoggle`, `/lay`, `/bellyflop`, and `/crawl`. Legend and higher can use `/spin`.

The pose commands are subject to the current location and protection rules. If a pose is denied, move to a safe unprotected location and try again.
