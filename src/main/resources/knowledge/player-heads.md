When InstaSkull is available to you, placed player heads and wall player heads break instantly. Mob heads, heads in inventories, and heads worn by entities are unaffected.

Normal claim protection and vanilla drops still apply, so an instant break does not bypass a protected area.

HeadDatabase is also installed. If `/hdb` or `/headdb` is available to your rank, its own help opens the searchable head database. That is separate from HauntedMC's published `/heads` rank perk; an installed plugin does not by itself mean every player has access.
