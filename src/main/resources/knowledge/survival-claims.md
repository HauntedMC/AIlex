The current Survival Rank page lists 25 claim regions, 3,000 starting Claimblocks, and 40 Claimblocks per online hour up to 10,000. It lists bonus Claimblocks of 20,000 for Elite, 40,000 for Legend, and 80,000 for Supreme; Supreme+ receives a further 15,000 monthly bonus.

Use `/claim`, `/newclaim`, or `/makeclaim` to create a claim. Use `/trust`, `/accesstrust`, `/containertrust`, `/permissiontrust`, and `/untrust` to manage access. Use `/claimslist` or `/listclaims` to view claims, `/trustlist` for a claim's trusted players, and `/unclaim`, `/abandonclaim`, `/declaim`, or `/removeclaim` to remove one.

Stand in the relevant claim before changing its trust. In normal GriefPrevention behaviour, `/trust <player>` gives building access; `/accesstrust <player>` gives access to buttons, levers, and beds; and `/containertrust <player>` additionally gives access to containers, animals, and crafting equipment. `/permissiontrust <player>` lets a trusted player manage claim trust. `/untrust <player>` removes that player's access, while `/untrust all` removes all trust from the claim. Other protection rules can still restrict an action.

For claim changes, use `/expandclaim`, `/extendclaim`, or `/resizeclaim`; `/subdivideclaims` or `/subdivideclaim` creates subclaims. The current page also lists `/trapped`, `/abandontoplevelclaim`, `/restrictsubclaim`, `/basicclaims`, and `/givepet <player>`. `/abandonallclaims` removes all your claims. The current claim tool and in-game feedback are authoritative for claim boundaries and permissions.
