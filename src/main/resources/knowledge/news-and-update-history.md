## August 2026 major network update
The newest reviewed large update introduced or documented a broad set of current systems: restart fallback plus automatic reconnect and opt-out, full-server queueing with `/queue leave`, delayed vote delivery while a target mode is unavailable, Command Scheduler events, protected DeathChests, AutoPickup replacing Drop2Inventory, `/msg mode` privacy choices, a modernized Lottery, FairPerks/CombatTag behaviour and related network quality-of-life work. The dedicated current feature articles contain the present-tense details.

## Dungeon update
A major Survival Dungeon announcement described separate replayable dungeon worlds with unique maps, exploration, combat, structures, challenging mobs and rewards. Players entered through `/warp dungeons`, chose dungeon NPCs and required matching Dungeon Keys. The feature addressed finite structure/loot availability in persistent worlds. These core concepts remain reflected in current reviewed Dungeon knowledge.

## Historical seasonal events
Archived HauntedMC news describes seasonal event-server experiences, including an Easter egg hunt where hidden player-head eggs awarded Cosmetic Fragments and a Halloween event running from 31 October through 2 November with team-based monster-wave survival, kits, unlockable locations/supply crates and multiple sessions/arenas. Those dates, rewards, team sizes and mechanics belong to the old event only and must not be presented as a current event.

## Historical TnTRun result
An archived TnTRun result named `Onlycraftkaz` first with 69 points, followed by `Salvadores` (48), `happyfoxyboy` (46), `Locoturco` (45) and `Silcrafter` (40), with historical Cosmetic Fragment/event-item prizes. This is server history, not a current leaderboard or reward table.

## Historical Minecraft 1.20.4 update
An old update announced all game modes moving from Minecraft 1.20.1 to 1.20.4 and discussed vanilla behaviour changes such as mob reach calculations. That version claim is obsolete for current compatibility.

## Historical Survival border expansion
An old Survival news item expanded the world-border radius from 16,000 to 18,000 blocks, describing a 36,000 by 36,000 area and newly generated 1.20-era content such as cherry groves/trees and trail ruins; it also referenced `/tppos`. This is historical world-generation/border information, not a current guaranteed border.

## HauntedMC 2.0 and other legacy updates
Older archive material references broader “HauntedMC 2.0” era renovations and many past event/help changes. When details conflict with current game modes, ranks, rules, commands or limits, the current reviewed corpus wins.
