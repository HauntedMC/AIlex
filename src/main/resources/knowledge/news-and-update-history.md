Historical chronology; dedicated current articles own present-tense facts.

- **Aug 2026 major update:** restart fallback/autoreconnect + opt-out, full-server queues (`/queue leave`), delayed target-specific vote delivery, Command Scheduler, DeathChests, AutoPickup replacing Drop2Inventory, `/msg mode`, modernized Lottery, FairPerks/CombatTag QoL.
- **Dungeon update:** replayable separate dungeon worlds/maps with exploration/combat/structures/mobs/rewards; `/warp dungeons`; NPC selection; matching Dungeon Keys; intended to avoid finite persistent-world structure/loot exhaustion.
- **Old Easter event:** hidden player-head eggs awarded Cosmetic Fragments.
- **Old Halloween event (31 Oct–2 Nov):** team monster-wave survival with kits, unlockable locations/supply crates, multiple sessions/arenas. Dates/rewards/team sizes/mechanics are historical only.
- **Old TnTRun result:** Onlycraftkaz 69, Salvadores 48, happyfoxyboy 46, Locoturco 45, Silcrafter 40; historical Cosmetic Fragment/event-item prizes.
- **Old 1.20.4 update:** moved game modes from 1.20.1→1.20.4 and mentioned vanilla changes such as mob reach; obsolete for current compatibility.
- **Old Survival border expansion:** radius 16,000→18,000 (36,000×36,000), new 1.20-era cherry groves/trees + trail ruins; referenced `/tppos`; not a guaranteed current border.
- Older “HauntedMC 2.0” renovations/history never override current modes/ranks/rules/commands/limits.
