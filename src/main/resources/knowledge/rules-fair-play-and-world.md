## Rule 5 — fair play
Players must play fairly and may not use tools that provide an unfair advantage. Hacked clients, X-ray, kill aura, movement/combat cheats such as unauthorized fly, speed or reach, autoclickers, gameplay-automation macros, bots/scripts, unauthorized minimap functions, unfair resource packs, tools that bypass server security or anticheat, and other unfair modifications are prohibited.

Performance or visual modifications such as FPS mods or shaders are generally allowed when they do not provide an unfair advantage. If uncertain about a mod, ask staff before using it; not knowing a rule does not guarantee a sanction will be removed.

## Rule 6 — bugs, glitches and exploits
Do not abuse bugs, glitches or exploits. Duplicating items, money, tokens, keys or other value is prohibited. Do not exploit plugin errors, glitch through walls/claims/protections/borders, bypass economy/cooldown/limit/protection systems, keep exploitable bugs secret for personal benefit, or spread exploit methods to others.

Report bugs to support or staff. Reporting after first abusing the bug does not erase the abuse.

## Rule 9 — farms, redstone and performance
Farms and redstone are allowed only while fair, reasonable and server-friendly. Lag machines are prohibited. Chunk loaders are prohibited unless a specific game mode explicitly allows them. Do not bypass idle kicks, limits or security systems. Constructions that generate extreme entity, tile-entity, hopper, redstone-update or other load are prohibited.

0-tick or exploit-like farms and duplication bugs are prohibited. Game-mode-specific farm rules also apply, and farms may not deliberately hinder others. Players must follow current entity, hopper, redstone and other limits published on the website or in-game. Staff may adapt, disable or remove a farm/build that causes excessive lag.

Older HauntedMC rules included exact numeric portal/farm limits and narrower special cases. Those old numbers are not the current general rule text; use `/limits` or a current game-mode rule before quoting a number.

## Rule 10 — AFK and automation
AFK play is allowed only within server rules and game-mode settings. Do not bypass idle kicks, automatically rejoin after an idle kick, use macros/bots/scripts to appear active, or have automated actions run while not actively playing. Do not use alt accounts to keep farms, chunks, mobs, events or other systems active.

One person may not use multiple accounts as if multiple people were actively playing to gain an unintended advantage. Inactive play may not produce an unfair automation benefit.

## Rule 11 — alt accounts
Alt accounts are allowed only when they are not used for abuse or unintended advantages. They may not evade bans/mutes; farm extra rewards, votes, keys, claims, plots or starter items; manipulate the economy; keep farms active; boost PvP/statistics/events; deceive players; or evade staff investigation. Staff can associate related accounts and apply measures to all involved accounts.

## Rule 14 — looting, structures and worlds
Use structures, loot and worlds socially so new players can also enjoy them. Large-scale stripping of structures, claiming/blocking structures merely to hinder others, treating important/rare structures as private property where that is not intended, claiming full biomes/large areas without an active project, and sabotaging others' discoveries/projects are prohibited.

Game-mode-specific rules may impose stricter handling of the Nether, End, villages, ancient cities, monuments, mansions, strongholds and other structures. Ask staff before claiming, destroying or fully looting an important or rare structure when unsure.
