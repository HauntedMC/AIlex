## Money commands
Documented economy commands include `/money` or `/balance`, `/pay`, `/paytoggle` and `/payconfirmtoggle`. The exact current balances of players are live/private state and must not be guessed.

## Player shops
Survival has documented player-shop/chest-shop systems and a mall route at `/warp mall`. Shop counts, rental duration, fees, stock, prices and whether a particular stall is available are dynamic. Store packages may also grant shop capacity; always use the live Store for current package details.

## Trading rules
Trading must be honest and clear. Scamming and misleading another player about price, contents, value or an agreement are prohibited. Bugs, exploits, alt accounts and bots may not be used to manipulate the economy. Clear agreements are recommended, but staff intervention does not guarantee every bad deal is reversed.

## Real-world value
In-game items, currencies, accounts, ranks and benefits may not be sold outside official systems for real money or external value. Trading HauntedMC value for real currency, gift cards, crypto or other external value is prohibited. Store products and virtual currencies have no real-world cash value and cannot be redeemed for money.
