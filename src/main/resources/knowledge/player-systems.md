- After death, items are kept in a virtual grave at the death location instead of dropping directly on the ground. Interact with it to recover items and experience; remaining items stay there if the inventory is full.
- AutoPickup replaced Drop2Inventory. Eligible Legend-rank players can toggle it with `/autopickup` for blocks they personally break. It does not overwrite items; excess drops fall normally if the inventory is full.
- Use `/msg mode` to choose who may send private messages: `FRIENDS` or `ALL`. `FRIENDS` is the default for new players.
- The Lottery has been modernized around tickets, the jackpot, donations, statistics and visible win chance. Use `/lottery buy` to buy a ticket, but never invent ticket prices, jackpot values, draw times or odds.
- FairPerks provides rank-based `/fly` and `/god`. Fly is automatically disabled when a player receives CombatTag. CombatTag tracks relevant combat and shows remaining time.
