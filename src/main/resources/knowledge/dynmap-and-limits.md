## Maps and Dynmap
HauntedMC has historically provided browser-based map/Dynmap functionality and exposes a player information route through `/maps`. Older official help documented 2D/3D map views, coordinates, world borders and overlays such as Survival claims or Creative plots, depending on the world/server.

Older help also documented `/dynmap hide` and `/dynmap show` for controlling map visibility. Because the current migrated Dynmap help page is only a placeholder, treat the feature as reviewed legacy/stable knowledge rather than inventing a current map URL. `/maps` or current in-game help is the preferred discovery route.

## Privacy on maps
Do not infer that every world shows player location, health, rank or name today just because an old Dynmap article did. Map layers and privacy settings can change. Respect the live map configuration.

## Limits
Current rules explicitly require players to obey entity, hopper, redstone and other limits published on the website or in game. `/limits` is the documented information route. Exact numeric limits are configuration-sensitive and must not be copied from old rule articles without current verification.

Old rules contained specific numeric restrictions such as portal counts and special farm constraints. Those old values are historical unless the current `/limits` page or game-mode rules repeat them.
