## Data HauntedMC may process
Depending on the service used, HauntedMC may process Minecraft username/UUID; website/community username; email; password hash; language/theme/preferences; roles/ranks/permissions/account status; linked accounts; IP address; join/leave times; chat and commands; gameplay data such as homes, claims, plots, rewards, votes, kits, cosmetics, tokens, keys, statistics and progress; moderation records; technical/security logs; support messages/evidence; website/device/browser/page-use data; and purchase-delivery references needed for support.

Passwords are not stored as readable plaintext when HauntedMC handles website passwords; they are protected as hashes. Full card/bank payment details are not processed by HauntedMC itself; checkout/payment processing is handled through Tebex/payment providers.

## Why data is processed
Purposes include operating the website/server, accounts and gameplay systems; delivering virtual benefits; handling support, complaints and appeals; rule enforcement; cheat/grief/spam/fraud/hack/DDoS prevention and investigation; security; troubleshooting; performance/stability/user-experience improvement; necessary administration; and legal obligations.

## Sharing and external services
Relevant providers can include hosting/infrastructure providers, Tebex and payment providers, Discord when used, email/support providers, security/anti-abuse/fraud services, technical logging/monitoring/backup services and competent authorities where legally required. External services have their own policies.

## Retention guidance
The current privacy policy says technical website/server logs are normally kept up to 90 days unless longer is needed for an incident, misuse or investigation; completed support tickets normally up to 24 months unless evidence/complaints/disputes require longer; account data while the account exists plus a reasonable period for security/recovery/disputes/legal duties; gameplay data as needed for gameplay/community/continuity; moderation data as needed for enforcement/appeals/repeat-abuse protection; purchase/delivery data as needed for delivery/support/admin/fraud/chargebacks/legal duties; cookie preferences up to 12 months unless changed/removed earlier. Serious incidents, fraud or disputes can justify longer retention where necessary.

## Privacy rights
Under the current policy, players may—depending on circumstances—request access, correction, deletion, restriction, data portability, objection to certain processing and withdrawal of consent. Submit privacy requests through support with subject **Privacy**. Identity verification may be required. Some data cannot be deleted immediately when still needed for security, moderation, fraud prevention, administration, law or disputes.

A player can also complain to the Dutch Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens. Players under 16 are advised to involve a parent/guardian before sharing personal data, creating an account, contacting support or purchasing.

## AIlex privacy behaviour
Haunty should avoid exposing another player's private data, private messages, moderation evidence, IP address, account details or hidden activity. For a privacy request, send the player to official support rather than pretending AIlex itself can exercise or adjudicate the legal request.
