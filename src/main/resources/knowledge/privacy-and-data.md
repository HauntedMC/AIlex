## Data/purposes
Depending on use, HauntedMC may process: Minecraft username/UUID; site/community username/email/password hash/preferences; roles/ranks/permissions/status/links; IP + join/leave; chat/commands; gameplay data (homes/claims/plots/rewards/votes/kits/cosmetics/tokens/keys/stats/progress); moderation; technical/security logs; support/evidence; website/device/browser/use data; purchase-delivery references. Passwords are hashed, not readable plaintext. Full card/bank details are handled by Tebex/payment providers, not HauntedMC.

Purposes: operate services/accounts/gameplay; deliver virtual benefits; support/complaints/appeals; rules/moderation; cheat/grief/spam/fraud/hack/DDoS prevention/investigation; security/troubleshooting/performance/UX; administration/legal duties.

Possible recipients/providers: hosting/infrastructure, Tebex/payment, Discord, email/support, anti-abuse/fraud/security, logging/monitoring/backup and legally competent authorities; external services have their own policies.

## Retention snapshot
| Data | Normal guidance |
|---|---|
| Technical website/server logs | up to 90 days |
| Completed support tickets | up to 24 months |
| Cookie preferences | up to 12 months unless changed/removed |
| Account/gameplay/moderation/purchase data | as needed for service, continuity, security, appeals, admin, fraud/chargebacks, disputes and legal duties |

Incidents, misuse, fraud, evidence or disputes may justify longer retention where necessary.

## Rights/safety
Depending on circumstances: access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability, objection, consent withdrawal. Use Support with subject **Privacy**; identity verification may be required. Necessary security/moderation/fraud/admin/legal/dispute data may not be deletable immediately. Complaints may go to the Dutch Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens. Under 16: involve parent/guardian before sharing personal data, account/support/purchase activity.

AIlex must not expose another player's private data/messages/moderation evidence/IP/account details/hidden activity and must route legal privacy requests to official Support.
