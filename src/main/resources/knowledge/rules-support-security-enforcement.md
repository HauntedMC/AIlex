## Rule 16 — support, reports and appeals
Use support, reports and appeals honestly. False reports, fabricated/manipulated evidence, staff spam, threats/insults in tickets, repeatedly resubmitting the same completed case, exposing another person's private information and pressuring staff for a different/faster decision are prohibited.

Appeal a ban, mute or other measure through the official support channel and remain honest and respectful. Filing an appeal does not automatically remove a measure.

## Rule 17 — privacy, safety and technical attacks
Protect your own privacy and others'. Sharing another person's real name, address, phone number, school details, photos or other private data without permission is prohibited. Doxing threats and using screenshots/conversations to harass someone are prohibited.

Do not ask people for accounts, passwords or personal data. Phishing, scam links, suspicious/malicious downloads, malware/spyware, DDoS attacks or threats, hacking attempts, bot attacks, crash clients, security-vulnerability abuse and unauthorized attempts to access databases/accounts/plugins/server files/systems are prohibited. Serious security incidents can lead to permanent exclusion and further action.

## Rule 18 — staff instructions and sanction evasion
Follow staff instructions. Do not fight about staff decisions in public chat; use support afterwards. During a ban, do not enter HauntedMC with another account. During a mute, do not use another account, another chat, signs, books, Discord linking or another mechanism to bypass the restriction. Evasion can extend or strengthen measures and can affect related accounts.

## Investigation and evidence
Staff may use logs, chat, commands, transactions, server data, screenshots, anticheat reports and other relevant information during investigations. HauntedMC does not always disclose all evidence, for example to protect privacy, reporters, staff methods or security/anticheat systems. A player may ask for an explanation through support but is not automatically entitled to all internal information.

## Possible measures
Measures can include warnings, kicks, temporary or permanent mutes, temporary or permanent bans, IP bans, content removal/rollback, withdrawal of items/money/ranks/perks obtained through abuse, restrictions on claims/plots/accounts/functions, event exclusion and restriction of support in cases of abuse. Severity depends on seriousness, intent, damage, repetition, evidence and circumstances.

The current rule text does **not** set the old blanket maximum of six months for temporary measures. A temporary measure can be short or long; a permanent measure has no automatic end date.

## Authority and latest version
Staff can act against clearly harmful, unfair, disruptive or unsafe behaviour even when the exact situation is not literally enumerated. Game-mode-specific rules apply in addition to general rules and a stricter game-mode rule takes precedence. The newest official rules on the website are authoritative.
