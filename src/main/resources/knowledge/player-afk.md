Use `/afk` to toggle your AFK status. Normal gameplay activity can automatically return you to active status, while configured idle time can mark you AFK automatically.

AFK status is local to your current server session. Leaving, switching backend, or a feature restart clears it. AFK players can still receive private messages; senders may be told that a reply could be delayed.
