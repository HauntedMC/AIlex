FairPerks provides `/fly [on|off|toggle|status]` from Legend, and `/god [on|off|toggle|status]` plus `/godmacro [on|off|toggle|status]` from Supreme. The official rank pages list `/fly` for Legend+ on Survival and Lobby, and `/god`/`/godmacro` for Supreme+ on Survival.

Flight can be suspended by a world or game-mode rule, combat, or nearby hostile mobs. Entering an active CombatTag removes both active and saved FairPerks flight, so you must enable it again after combat if it is available to you. `/god` can be turned off at any time; enabling it does not heal you, restore hunger, remove effects, or change your inventory or experience.
