The current Survival Rank page lists Silk-Touch spawner mining from Legend. Where permitted, Silk Touch lets you pick up a placed mob spawner and preserve its mob type. You need Silk Touch in your main hand, permission to mine it, an allowed spawner type, and one completely empty inventory slot. The empty-slot rule does not use spare room in an existing stack.

Place a typed spawner normally to restore its stored mob type, subject to the current placement rules. Right-click a placed spawner with your main hand to toggle it on or off where SpawnerToggle is available. A disabled spawner keeps its normal settings but stops spawning; the off state normally persists through world saves and restarts. The feature can be enabled outside Survival even though the current rank entitlement is published on the Survival page.
