Deep Haste can strengthen a Haste effect supplied by a beacon while you are below the configured depth. Only beacon Haste is affected; potion, command, and other Haste effects are unchanged.

Moving above the configured depth removes the temporary boosted effect. Let the beacon apply Haste again if you need its normal effect to return.
