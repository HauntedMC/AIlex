The current Creative Rank page lists 2 plots for Speler, 6 for Elite, 8 for Legend, and 20 for Supreme and Supreme+. Legend and higher can merge up to four plots in a cluster.

Use `/plot claim`, `/plot auto`, `/plot home`, `/plot tp`, `/plot visit`, `/plot list`, `/plot info`, or `/plot delete` to manage your plots. Use `/plot trust <player>`, `/plot add <player>`, `/plot remove <player>`, `/plot deny <player>`, and `/plot kick <player>` to manage other players.

Other published plot tools include `/plot clear`, `/plot sethome`, `/plot setbiome <biome>`, `/plot setdescription <text>`, `/plot flag`, `/plot music`, `/plot comment`, `/plot inbox`, and `/plot help`. Use `/plot merge auto`, `/plot merge all`, or `/plot merge <direction>` from Legend, and `/plot unlink` to undo a merge.
