Use `/balloons` to choose a balloon and `/balloons remove` to remove it. The menu only shows balloons you are allowed to use.

Balloons are temporary local cosmetics. They are removed when you die, enter a vehicle, leave the server, or switch backend, so select one again after returning. A custom-model balloon may require the matching resource pack to look correct.
