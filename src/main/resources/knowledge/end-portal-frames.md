Where permitted, you can pick up and place End Portal Frames outside natural strongholds. Picking up a frame drops a normal frame item and clears nearby active portal blocks on the same level.

This feature does not create an End portal for you; normal Minecraft placement and activation rules still apply. Protection can restrict frame pickup or placement.
