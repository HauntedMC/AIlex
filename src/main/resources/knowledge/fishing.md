HauntedMC has EvenMoreFish installed. Use `/emf` for the player help that is enabled for you. EvenMoreFish can provide custom catches, fishing competitions, baits, a fish shop, rewards, leaderboards, and a catch journal; their availability and rewards are set by the server.

When those options are enabled, useful commands include `/emf top` for leaderboards, `/emf journal [rarity]` for your collection, `/emf sellall` for eligible fish, and `/emf toggle bossbar|fishing|catchMessage` for display preferences. Check `/emf` before relying on an option: a command can be absent or permission-restricted on HauntedMC.
