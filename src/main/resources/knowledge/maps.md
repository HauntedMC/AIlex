The official Dynmap help page links to the available Survival and Creative maps. Maps can show coordinates, player location/health/rank/name, world borders, claims or plots, spawn, and 2D/3D views when enabled. Use `/dynmap hide` to hide yourself and `/dynmap show` to become visible again.
