## What the store offers
The HauntedMC Store sells digital/virtual benefits for HauntedMC. Current store families include ranks and rank upgrades, Supreme+ offerings, Survival-related benefits such as claimblocks/chest-shop/home capacity, Creative plot/merge-related benefits, cosmetics and other virtual packages. Product inventory changes; do not invent a package that is not currently shown.

The official store is `https://store.hauntedmc.nl/`. Purchases are for use on HauntedMC (`play.hauntedmc.nl`).

## Prices and promotions
Prices, discounts, bundle contents and limited-time offers can change. Never answer a current price from a cached knowledge article. Use the current Store. A successful prior order normally keeps its accepted price; obvious pricing/configuration errors can be corrected under the applicable terms.

## Checkout and delivery
The Store uses Tebex for webstore checkout, payment processing and delivery. Before paying, players should verify Minecraft username, product, relevant game mode, amount, discount, currency, payment method and checkout terms.

Digital benefits are normally linked to the Minecraft username or UUID entered at purchase and are usually delivered automatically after successful payment. Delivery can be delayed by technical/store/server/network issues, fraud checks, Tebex/payment-provider issues or an incorrect username. Some rank/permission changes may require reconnecting before everything is visible.

For a delivery problem, support may need the Minecraft username, order number/transaction reference, date, product, explanation and—if requested—screenshots/payment evidence.

## Virtual value and rules
Store products are virtual benefits. They have no real cash value, cannot be redeemed for money and may not be sold/rented/traded outside HauntedMC for real-world value. A purchase never exempts a player from server rules. Benefits obtained through abuse/fraud/exploits can be withdrawn.

HauntedMC may reasonably change ranks, perks, permissions, economy, game modes, plugins or related benefits for balance, technical changes, security, performance, abuse prevention, Minecraft/plugin changes, rules/law compliance or server improvement. Major changes are normally communicated when reasonable.

## Refunds, withdrawal and chargebacks
Digital content is often delivered immediately or shortly after payment, so refunds can be limited. Correctly delivered purchases are normally not refunded except where applicable Tebex/payment terms or mandatory consumer rights require it. A ban/mute for rule violations does not automatically create a refund right.

Chargebacks/payment disputes can lead to temporary or permanent restrictions while investigated. Mandatory consumer rights are not waived by the HauntedMC summary. The checkout terms in force for the actual purchase matter.

## Minors
Current HauntedMC store terms tell players under 16 to get permission/help from a parent or guardian before purchasing. External checkout/provider terms can also impose their own age requirements.

## Support
Use `https://hauntedmc.nl/support` for HauntedMC purchase/delivery questions. Payment/checkout disputes may also require Tebex or the payment provider. Do not request full payment-card or bank details in game chat.
