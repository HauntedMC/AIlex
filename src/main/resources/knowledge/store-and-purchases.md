Official Store: `https://store.hauntedmc.nl/`; purchases apply to `play.hauntedmc.nl`. Product families include ranks/upgrades/Supreme+, Survival capacities (claimblocks/chest shops/homes), Creative plot/merge benefits, cosmetics and other virtual packages. Inventory/prices/discounts/bundles/promotions are live; use the Store. Accepted prior order price normally stands, except obvious pricing/config errors under applicable terms.

## Checkout/delivery
Tebex handles checkout/payment/delivery. Before paying verify Minecraft username, product/game mode, quantity, discount, currency, method and terms. Benefits link to the entered username/UUID and usually deliver automatically after successful payment; delays can result from technical/network/store issues, fraud checks, Tebex/provider issues or wrong username. Some permissions/ranks may require reconnect.

Support may need username, order/transaction reference, date, product, explanation and requested screenshots/payment evidence; never request full card/bank details in game chat.

## Value/changes
Benefits are virtual: no cash value/redemption and no off-platform real-value sale/rental/trade. Purchases do not exempt players from rules; abuse/fraud/exploit benefits can be withdrawn. HauntedMC may reasonably change ranks/perks/permissions/economy/modes/plugins/benefits for balance, technical/security/performance/abuse prevention, Minecraft/plugin changes, compliance or improvement; major changes are normally communicated when reasonable.

## Refunds/disputes/minors
Immediate digital delivery can limit refunds. Correctly delivered purchases are normally non-refundable except where Tebex/payment terms or mandatory consumer rights require otherwise; bans/mutes do not automatically create refund rights. Chargebacks/disputes may cause temporary/permanent restrictions during investigation. Purchase-time checkout terms and mandatory rights control.

Under 16: get parent/guardian permission/help; provider terms may add age requirements. HauntedMC support: `https://hauntedmc.nl/support`; payment disputes may also require Tebex/payment provider.
