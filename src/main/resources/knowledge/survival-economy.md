Use `/money` or `/balance` to view money, `/money <player>` to view a player's balance when allowed, and `/pay <player>` to pay another player. Use `/paytoggle` and `/payconfirmtoggle` to manage the current payment preferences.

The current Survival Rank page lists online Token rewards every 20 minutes: 30 for Speler, 50 for Elite, 60 for Legend, 100 for Supreme, and 150 for Supreme+. Current balances and payment rules in game are authoritative.
