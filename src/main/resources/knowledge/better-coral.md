When BetterCoral is active, live vanilla coral stays alive outside water instead of drying out through the normal vanilla fade. This covers coral blocks, plants, floor fans, and wall fans.

The server can also offer furnace recipes that turn live coral items into their matching dead variants. World-editing or another plugin directly replacing coral can still change it normally.
