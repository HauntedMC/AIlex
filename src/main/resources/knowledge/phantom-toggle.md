Use `/phantomtoggle`, `/phantomtoggle on`, `/phantomtoggle off`, `/phantomtoggle toggle`, or `/phantomtoggle status` to control whether vanilla insomnia phantoms may spawn for you, when the perk is available.

This affects only future natural insomnia phantoms. It does not remove phantoms already present, stop manually or custom-spawned phantoms, reset insomnia, or protect you from an existing phantom. Your preference normally persists locally with your player data.
