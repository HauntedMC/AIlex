Liquid Tanks are special hopper-based storage blocks for resources such as water, lava, milk, soups, honey, dragon breath, and experience. Their contents persist as long as the tank remains valid.

An experience tank can exchange bottles in complete seven-XP units and can automatically move experience for nearby Survival or Adventure players according to the current setup. Placing and using tanks can be permission- or limit-controlled.
