Use `/warps` to see the live Survival warp list and `/warp <name>` to travel to one. The published list includes animalfarm, bed, crates, dropparty, enchant, end, enderdragon, mobfarm, nether, partymine, portals, repair, servershop, shop, and squidfarm.

The current rank page lists `/warp farm` from Elite. A warp can be temporarily unavailable or restricted by a live game-mode rule, so `/warps` is the final source of truth.
