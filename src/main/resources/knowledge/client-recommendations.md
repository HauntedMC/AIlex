Use `/clientinfo` or `/clientinfo info` to view the client information that is available to you. Use `/clientinfo recommend` to see available recommendations for client settings such as view distance, chat mode, or particles. Use `/clientinfo toggle` to turn automatic advice on or off, and `/clientinfo help` for the command help.

Recommendations can change by game mode. They are advice, not a requirement and not an anti-cheat judgement.
