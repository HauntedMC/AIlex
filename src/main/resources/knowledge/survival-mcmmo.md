Use `/mctop <skill>` for a skill leaderboard, `/mcrank` for your ranking, `/mcstats` for your stats, and `/mcinfo` or `/mcmmo help` for help. Use `/party` for McMMO parties and `/redeem` for available McMMO credits.

For a party, start with `/party ?` to see the subcommands currently available to you. mcMMO normally supports creating or joining a party, inviting players, party chat, and leaving it; sharing, teleporting, passwords, alliances, and level requirements are server-configured. Do not assume that a party option from another server is enabled here.

You can also use a skill's own command for its abilities. Check the in-game McMMO help for the exact available skills and their requirements.
