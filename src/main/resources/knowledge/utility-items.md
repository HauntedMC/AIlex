Some servers can give you utility items shortly after you join. Right-clicking one runs its configured player command, such as opening a server menu.

Utility items can be locked, unmovable, or undroppable. That behaviour is intentional: use the item rather than trying to place, move, or drop it. The exact items and their actions depend on the server you joined.
