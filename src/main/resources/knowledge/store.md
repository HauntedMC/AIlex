Use `https://store.hauntedmc.nl/` for current products, prices, checkout, and store terms. Check your Minecraft username and basket before paying. The store says completed payments are applied to the HauntedMC servers and may require you to rejoin before rank functions appear. For rank or purchase questions, contact `support@hauntedmc.nl`. Current checkout terms control purchases.

The Global Rank page lists `/store`, `/buy`, and `/winkel` as player store commands.
