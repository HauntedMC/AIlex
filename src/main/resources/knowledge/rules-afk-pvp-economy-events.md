## Rule 12 — PvP, deathtraps and teleport abuse
PvP must remain fair. Spawn killing is prohibited. Combat logging is prohibited when the relevant game mode forbids it. Do not deceive players into deathtraps, kill players outside allowed PvP areas, abuse teleport requests to kill players, boost by repeatedly arranging kills, use hacks/macros in PvP, or use PvP for sustained intimidation/harassment.

Do not teleport players into lava, the void, traps or mobs. Homes, warps and teleport requests may not be used to kill players. Do not imprison players, hide dangerous intent behind unclear/false teleport requests, or build traps at public places, warps, claims or routes. Game-mode-specific PvP rules can add stricter requirements.

## Rule 13 — economy, trade and scamming
Trade must be honest and clear. Scamming and misrepresentation about price, contents, value or agreements are prohibited. Do not abuse chargebacks/payment issues, bugs, exploits, alts or bots for economic advantage.

In-game items, currency, accounts, ranks or benefits may not be sold for real money outside official systems. Real currency, gift cards, crypto or other external value may not be traded for HauntedMC items. Make clear trade agreements. Staff may act on abuse, but not every bad deal is automatically rolled back.

## Rule 15 — events
General server rules and any event-specific rules apply. Cheating, bug exploitation, sabotaging an event, unfairly helping teams/participants, farming event rewards with alt accounts, ignoring staff instructions and hindering participants outside normal event gameplay are prohibited. Staff may disqualify participants or withdraw rewards for abuse.

Event schedules, team sizes, kits, maps, reward amounts and sign-up procedures are time-bound. Use the current event announcement rather than an old event article.
