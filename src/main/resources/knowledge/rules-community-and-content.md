These are summaries of the current official server rules, version 1.0.0 dated 23 June 2026. The newest rule page always wins if it changes.

## Rule 1 — respectful behaviour
Treat players, staff and the community with respect. Insults, bullying, harassment, intimidation and deliberately bothering people are prohibited. Discrimination, racism, sexism, homophobia, transphobia and other hateful remarks are prohibited. Threats, doxing and sharing private information are prohibited. Sexual, extremely violent or otherwise inappropriate remarks are prohibited.

Spam, flooding, caps spam and repeated messages are prohibited. Deliberate provocation, toxic behaviour and seeking fights are prohibited. Private messages, party chat, signs, books, item names and other communication channels may not be abused. If another player says a joke or interaction should stop, stop it.

## Rule 2 — language
Dutch is HauntedMC's main language and English is also allowed. Public chat must use a language staff can reasonably moderate. If staff asks players to use Dutch or English, follow that instruction. Older rules that automatically tolerated German are superseded by this current wording.

## Rule 3 — advertising and promotion
Advertising other Minecraft servers is prohibited. Do not share server IPs, invite links or promotions for other servers. Advertising websites, stores, Discord servers, social media or services without permission is prohibited, as is deliberately trying to pull players away from HauntedMC.

Covert advertising through nicknames, signs, books, builds, item names or private messages is also prohibited. Official HauntedMC pages, vote links and HauntedMC content may be linked as long as it is not spammed.

## Rule 4 — usernames, nicknames, skins and visible profiles
Usernames, nicknames, skins, profiles, item names and visible content must be suitable for the community. Inappropriate, insulting, discriminatory or sexual names/skins are prohibited. Do not bypass filters, imitate staff or known players to create confusion, impersonate people, use unreadable symbol-only nicknames, or use identity/content to troll or scam.

Staff can require a player to change an unsuitable name, nickname, skin or profile. Refusing can lead to measures.

## Rule 7 — griefing, claims and ownership
Griefing is prohibited. Do not destroy or alter someone else's build without permission, empty containers you should not have access to, block another person's claim/plot/island/town/build, claim someone else's build, or use lava, water, mobs, redstone or other mechanisms to cause damage. Deliberately ruining someone else's environment or project is prohibited.

Players are responsible for who they trust in claims, plots, islands, towns, teams and other protected environments. Technical access does not make clear abuse or scamming acceptable; staff can still intervene.

## Rule 8 — building and visible content
Builds and placed content must be suitable for the community. Discriminatory, hateful or sexually explicit builds are prohibited, as are symbols/references intended to shock, intimidate or insult. Builds may not deceive or harm players.

Lag machines and excessively demanding redstone, farms or entity systems are prohibited. Builds may not block claims, routes, warps, spawn areas or server functions. Large areas should not be claimed without a reasonably active project. Staff may adapt, remove or move builds when rules, performance, safety or community interests require it.
