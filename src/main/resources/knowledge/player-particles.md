HauntedMC has PlayerParticles installed. When particles are available to you, use `/pp` to open its controls; the plugin normally opens its player GUI from that command. Effects, styles, colours, locations, unlock requirements, and any cost are server-configured, so only the live menu tells you what you can select.

If `/pp` says you lack permission or does not open, that does not mean the command is misspelled. The current rank or event access may not include the feature; do not use commands intended to change another player's particles.
