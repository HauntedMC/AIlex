The current Survival Rank page lists an Armor Stand Editor, opening shulker boxes with Shift-click, and CraftBook sign perks. The current rank, world, and protection rules decide whether each interaction is usable where you are.

For the Armor Stand Editor, HauntedMC's published perk uses flint. Hold flint and interact with the armour stand when permitted; normal claim and plot protection still applies. `/ase ?` can show the installed editor's own help, but it does not grant access.

CraftBook sign mechanisms are configurable. Its normal bridge layout uses `[Bridge]` on the second line and can toggle by sign interaction or redstone, but that generic layout is not proof that HauntedMC lets your rank create it or that every block type works. Follow the live rank information and in-game feedback for bridges, lifts, gates, teleport signs, payment signs, and redstone signs.
