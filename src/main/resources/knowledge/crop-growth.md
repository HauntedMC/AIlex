HauntedMC has CropChecker installed. It can show the growth progress of a crop or the honey level of a beehive. Try the current server interaction with a hoe on the crop or hive; the result may appear in a scoreboard, action bar, or another live display.

The required tool, click type, worlds, and display style are server settings. CropChecker only reports a state: it does not speed up growth, make a crop harvestable, or bypass claim protection.
