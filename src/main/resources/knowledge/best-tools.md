HauntedMC has BestTools installed. Use `/besttools` or `/bt` to toggle automatic selection of the best available tool when you break a block. The plugin normally takes tool speed and enchantments into account. Use `/besttools hotbar` to switch between searching only your hotbar and searching your full inventory.

Use `/refill` or `/rf` to toggle automatic refilling of an empty hotbar stack from your inventory. These controls do not create items and do not override a protected area, a current game-mode rule, or a missing permission. The live response tells you whether each option is available.
