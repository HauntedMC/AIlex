Use `/vote` to open HauntedMC's official vote page, `/vote links` to see the vote links, and `/vote leaderboard` for the vote leaderboard. Use `/vote remind on`, `/vote remind off`, or `/vote remind toggle` to control vote reminders.

Each listed site can be used once a day. The published reset times are 00:00 for MinecraftKrant and MineServers and 06:00 for Minecraft-MP. A vote gives Survival 1 Vote Key (for `/warp crates`), 250 Tokens, 1 McMMO Credit, and 80 Claimblocks, plus 10 Cosmetic Fragments in the lobby. Votes can be retained for up to 24 hours when a target game mode is unavailable. Use the live vote page for current links, ranking, prizes, and delivery status.
