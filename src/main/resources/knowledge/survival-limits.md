Per base, the documented container limit is 2,000 hoppers and 2,000 chests/other containers. Per cluster: animals 20, mobs 15, villagers 8, bees 9 with 25 free blocks, allays 5 with 25 free blocks, and hostile mobs in farm clusters 5 with at least 3 blocks between clusters.

| Rank | Spawners |
|---|---:|
| Speler | 2 |
| Elite | 5 |
| Legend | 15 |
| Supreme | 25 |

Tagged pets/mobs count. `/limits` and live configuration override this snapshot.
