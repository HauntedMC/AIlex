HauntedMC has ToolStats installed. It can add item lore that records activity and origin on the item itself: for example blocks mined, crops harvested, fish caught, mob or player kills, damage, flight time, or whether the tool was crafted, found, looted, or traded. Which statistics appear, and whether an item needs a token first, are live server settings.

If the lore on the item in your hand is wrong and `/toolstats reset` is available to you, it rebuilds the lore from the item's recorded data. It does not reset the statistics. Do not use a purge/remove option merely to tidy an item: such options can delete its ToolStats data and are normally restricted.
