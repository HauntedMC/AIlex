HauntedMC has MineResetLite installed. A designated mine area can regenerate its blocks automatically and can warn players before a reset. If a mine announces that it will reset, leave the area and do not place valuables or make a base there; the reset is meant to replace the mine's block area.

The locations, warning messages, compositions, and reset times are configured per mine and must be learned from the live mine signage, announcement, or player-facing instructions. `/mrl` management commands are not player help and do not establish which mines HauntedMC currently operates.
