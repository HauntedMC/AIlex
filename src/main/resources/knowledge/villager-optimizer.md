When VillagerOptimizer is active, use an Emerald Block on a villager to toggle its awareness where protection allows. A disabled villager can be less active for performance reasons.

Interact with a disabled villager to let the server handle configured restocking and trading behaviour. Claim protection still applies at the villager's location.
