The current Survival Rank page lists player-shop limits of 5 for Speler, 25 for Elite, 35 for Legend, and 100 for Supreme and Supreme+. At `/warp mall`, use `/arm addmember`, `/arm removemember`, and `/arm tp shop<number>` for the published shop-management flow.

HauntedMC also has QuickShop-Hikari installed. Where player QuickShops are enabled, the normal flow is to place the allowed shop container, create the shop using the in-game prompt, and set its price. Buyers normally left-click to buy; owners use the shop's control menu to view or change its live sell/buy, price, stock, or frozen state. A shop can be out of stock or unavailable even when its container remains.

Shop availability, container types, rent, stock, limits, and permission rules are live. Check the mall or server-shop instructions before opening or changing a shop.
