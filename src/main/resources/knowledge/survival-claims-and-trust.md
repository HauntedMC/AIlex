## Creating and finding claims
Documented claim entry points are `/claim`, `/newclaim` and `/makeclaim`. `/claimslist` lists a player's claims and `/claimtutorial` is the public tutorial route where available. `/trapped` is the documented escape route when a player is stuck in a claim.

Claim-block totals, accrual rates and purchased claim-block packages are variable. Never invent an amount. Use `/limits`, `/ranks`, the current store or live claim state where available.

## Trust levels
- `/trust <name>` — broader build/use trust.
- `/containertrust <name>` — container-oriented trust.
- `/accesstrust <name>` — access-oriented trust.
- `/permissiontrust <name>` — permission-management trust.
- `/untrust <name>` — revoke trust.
- `/trustlist` — inspect trusted players.

Only describe the exact permission boundary of a trust level when current help/live plugin evidence provides it; the names above are canonical commands, but implementations can evolve.

## Responsibility and abuse
Current rules say players are responsible for whom they trust in protected environments, but staff may still intervene in clear abuse, scamming or damage even if technical access existed. Claim access is never permission to grief, scam or deliberately harm another player's project.

Large areas or whole biomes must not be claimed merely to block others without a reasonably active project. Claims/builds may be changed or removed by staff when they violate rules, performance, safety or community interests.
