The current Survival Rank page lists these utility commands from Elite: `/compass`, `/getpos`, `/near`, `/ptime`, `/pweather`, `/skull <name>`, `/heads`, `/top`, `/enderchest`, `/workbench`, `/loom`, `/trash`, `/stonecutter`, `/cartographytable`, `/grindstone`, `/smithingtable`, `/anvil`, `/hat`, and `/jump`.

From Supreme, the published page adds `/condense` or `/blocks`, `/veinminer`, `/speed walk <number>`, and `/speed fly <number>`. Commands can be disabled in specific worlds or while another current gameplay rule applies.

The same current page lists `/xp`, `/unlock`, `/unlockchest`, and `/trade <player>` for all Survival players. Lottery commands are documented separately because their limits and draw state are live.
