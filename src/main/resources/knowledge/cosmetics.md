Open cosmetic crates in the lobby and use `/cosmetics` to choose cosmetics. The help page describes four crate tiers and Cosmetic Fragments: 10 for voting, 25 per online hour, and 200 for a successful referral; the referred player receives 400 fragments. Current crate contents, costs, and store availability are shown in the live menu and store.
