Matching vanilla double doors can open and close together. Left-click a door to knock on it. Trapdoors and fence gates are not part of this feature.

Door synchronization only applies to correctly paired doors of the same material and facing, with opposite hinges. Your original interaction must pass normal protection checks. If a paired door crosses a claim or region boundary and behaves unexpectedly, stop using it and report it to staff.
